The line

Every app, one stop at a time

Start with the daily quiz, then ride on.

  1. Stop 1 · Every day ChemTrain Daily A new quiz every day with questions from every app, the same for everyone. Plus problem sets you can share with a class. 2260 questions in the daily pool
  2. Stop 2 · No mistakes allowed Beast mode Questions from every app keep coming until your first mistake. How far can you go? 2260 questions to get through
  3. Stop 3 Reaction Learn Named reactions and reagents: products, names, conditions and what they do. 281 cards
  4. Stop 4 pKa Learn pKa values of acids and bases, and the most acidic proton of a molecule. 240 cards
  5. Stop 5 PG Learn Protecting groups and the conditions that remove them. 44 cards
  6. Stop 6 TMC Learn Electron counting, oxidation states, dⁿ and shapes of transition-metal complexes. 246 cards
  7. Stop 7 Radical Learn Nucleophilic and electrophilic radicals, polarity matching, and where radicals add and abstract H. 50 questions
  8. Stop 8 Photoredox Learn Redox potentials of photocatalysts, reagents and substrates, electron transfer and photoredox cycles. 143 cards
  9. Stop 9 Reactivity Learn Nu/E Mayr nucleophilicity and electrophilicity, the most reactive atom, and regioselectivity. 240 cards

On your phone

Take ChemTrain with you

Install it once and every app is on your phone under one icon. It opens full screen and works without internet.

You are using the installed ChemTrain. Every app is kept on this device, so it works without internet.

  • Every app and the daily quiz, offline (about 22 MB)
  • Free: no account, no ads, nothing to sign up for
  • Your progress is saved on your device, not on a server
  • Updates arrive by themselves when you are online
  1. Open this page in Safari (on iOS 16.4 or later, Chrome and Edge work too).
  2. Tap Share , in the bar at the bottom (at the top on iPad).
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, then Add.
  4. Open ChemTrain from your home screen. The first time, it saves every app, so stay online for a minute.

On iPhone the installed app keeps its own progress: what you did in Safari stays in Safari.

  1. Open this page in Chrome (or Edge, Samsung Internet, Firefox).
  2. Tap Install when it is offered, or open the menu ⋮ and choose Install app (or Add to Home screen).
  3. Open ChemTrain from your home screen. Every app is saved for offline use.
  1. In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar, or choose Install in the browser's menu.
  2. In Safari on a Mac, choose File → Add to Dock.
  3. ChemTrain opens in its own window and works offline.
